The Problem

You Didn't Become a Consultant
to Drown in Lab PDFs

Every project starts the same way. A lab sends over raw data files — sometimes 60, 80, even 120 pages long — packed with mixed tables, QC samples, method blanks, surrogates, and detection limits that have nothing to do with your specific site. Somewhere buried in all of that noise are the values you actually need.

You open the PDF. You start scrolling. You start copying. You start second-guessing whether the value in row 14 of Table 3 matches the CAS number you entered two cells back. You switch between windows, cross-referencing against your spreadsheet, your checklist, your memory of what the last report looked like.

Three hours later, you have a draft — and you still haven't checked it.

Dozens of Pages, Most of It Irrelevant

Raw lab deliverables are bloated. You're manually extracting data from files designed for lab QA/QC — not for TCEQ APAR formatting. Standard tools choke on the volume or can't parse the structure at all.

Manual Entry Means Manual Errors

CAS numbers transposed. Concentrations misread. Units missed during conversion. Every manual keystroke is a liability — especially when your name is on the report and the deadline was yesterday.

Formatting Overhead Eats Your Margins

Even after the data is correct, you still need to format the report. Match your firm's layout. Match TCEQ's expectations. Match the look of every report your client has seen before. That's billable hours burned on presentation, not analysis.

How It Works

Three Steps. One Upload.
A Complete APAR Report.

Apar-Intelligence is purpose-built for one workflow: transforming raw analytical lab data into TCEQ-compliant APAR reports. No general-purpose spreadsheet hacks. No workarounds. Just a direct, auditable pipeline from raw file to finished document.

01

Select Your Page Ranges

Open the upload interface and specify exactly which page ranges contain the relevant analytical data. The system only processes what you designate — no bloated parsing, no wasted compute, no surprises from extraneous lab QC pages contaminating your results.

02

Upload & Let It Parse

Drop your raw lab PDF into the secure upload queue. Apar-Intelligence extracts the analytical values, compound identifiers, CAS numbers, method references, and reporting limits from your selected ranges — structuring them into an organized, queryable dataset in real time.

03

Review, Cross-Check, Generate

Use the built-in cross-check dashboard to verify every extracted value against your source file. Fill in missing CAS numbers from the integrated database. Then generate your APAR report in your firm's exact format — ready for review, ready for submission.

Cross-Check Database

Every extracted value is placed into a structured, side-by-side database that mirrors your source PDF. You see the original lab value on the left and the extracted value on the right — aligned row by row, compound by compound.

When the lab report is missing a CAS number or uses an uncommon analyte name, the system flags it and suggests matches from an integrated chemical reference database. You confirm or override in one click. No separate lookups. No switching to a browser tab to search a CAS registry.

  • Side-by-side value comparison against source PDF
  • Automatic CAS number lookup for flagged compounds
  • Override and annotation capability for consultant review
  • Audit trail documenting every value confirmation
